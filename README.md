# John: Rogue Commando 💥 (Open-World & Horde Survival Edition)

An adrenaline-pumping, full-screen retro arcade action shooter built with HTML5 Canvas, JavaScript, and Web Audio API.

Powered by original character sprites, ambient art, and soundtrack from **[@precidoboss](https://github.com/precidoboss)**!

Play instantly in your browser via **GitHub Pages**:

---

## 🕹️ Two Exciting Game Modes

### 1. 🗺️ Open-World Infiltration Campaign
* Traverse a massive seamless 4,200px world spanning 4 distinct biomes (Delta Outpost, Toxic Jungle, Crystal Vaults, and Cyber-Citadel).
* Activate ground-level **Checkpoint Beacons** that fully heal John to 100% HP & Shields and unlock instant fast-travel.
* Infiltrate the fortress and defeat the 1,800 HP **Apex Mech Titan**!

### 2. ⚡ Endless Horde Survival (New Mode!)
* Fight off continuous, escalating waves of enemy squads in a frantic combat arena.
* Track your wave records, hostiles remaining, and combo multipliers.
* Defeat waves to earn bonus Cyber-Scrap caches!

## 🕹️ Controls Quick Reference

| Action | Keyboard | Mobile / Touch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shoot** | **`SPACEBAR`** / `J` / `Z` / `F` / **Left Click** | Red Fire Button (💥) |
| **Jump** | **`W`** / **`ArrowUp`** | Green Jump Button (▲) |
| **Move Left / Right** | `A` / `D` or `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` | Left / Right D-Pad Buttons |
| **Crouch** | `S` / `ArrowDown` | Down D-Pad Button (▼) |
| **Tactical Dash / Dodge** | `Shift` (Left or Right) | Purple Dash Button (⚡) |
| **Reload** | `R` | Orange Reload Button (⟳) |
| **Switch Weapon** | `Q` / `E` or `1`, `2`, `3`, `4` | Blue Switch Button (⇄) |
| **Open Menu / Pause** | `M` / `ESC` | Top Bar / Touch Menu |

## 👾 High-Visibility Enemies & Threat AI

Every enemy is prominently rendered with overhead threat badges, health meters, and glowing visors:

* **Cyborg Heavy Troopers**: Front-line soldiers that advance, aim laser-guided rifles, and fire 3-round bursts.
* **Bio-Mutant Arachnids**: Fast-scuttling creatures with animated legs that leap at John.
* **Aerial Gunship Drones**: Hovering drones with twin spinning rotor blades and homing plasma bolts.
* **Citadel Beam Snipers**: Stationary marksmen that project warning laser sights before unleashing high-speed rail slugs.
* **Apex Mech Titan Boss**: Multi-stage mechanical giant with missile barrages and dynamic boss battle music (`snd_boss_music.ogg`)!

---

## ⚙️ Cyber-Armory & Weapons Arsenal

* **Viper Assault Rifle**: Automatic rifle with ejected brass shell casings.
* **Hydra Plasma Shotgun**: 5-pellet spread tearing through close-range bio-swarms.
* **Zeus Pierce Railgun**: Pierces multiple enemies in a single beam.
* **Titan Rocket Launcher**: Long-range explosive ordnance with AoE shockwaves.
